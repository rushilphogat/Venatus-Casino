# Game table reference notes

These sources describe digital implementations of the five Venatus variants. The observations come from published controls, illustrated examples, and official help or manuals. The linked products were not played during this review. Animation timings and sounds in Venatus are its own design choices.

## Texas Hold’em

PokerStars illustrates the board at each betting street. Replay Poker documents how changing a preset, dragging the slider, or typing an amount previews a wager before the player presses Raise. Keep the board anchored, distinguish the amount to call from the total raise, and display the award alongside each revealed hand. [PokerStars illustrated Hold’em tutorial](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/texas-holdem-rules/) · [Replay Poker betting controls](https://replayhelp.casino.org/hc/en-us/articles/360001879294-Betting-and-raising)

## Indian Rummy

RummyCircle describes a dedicated Sort action at the start of the hand. RummyEasy places a named open discard beside the closed stock and illustrates valid and invalid groups using individual card faces. Keep both piles directly actionable, preserve the player’s hand arrangement between draws, and show which groups contributed to a declaration. [RummyCircle game guide](https://www.rummycircle.com/how-to-play-rummy/rummy-rules.html) · [RummyEasy illustrated turns and melds](https://rummyeasy.com/how-to-play)

## Oh Hell

CardGames.io places the trump suit in the bid prompt and identifies players across the score table. Trickster explains how the turned trump and changing hand size affect each bid. Keep the current round, available tricks and trump in view through bidding. Identify the trick winner at their seat and retain the last result while the next player acts. [CardGames.io playable interface](https://cardgames.io/ohhell/) · [Trickster Oh Hell game help](https://www.trickstercards.com/home/help/oh-hell-basics.html)

## Bluff

SnackTurn illustrates the difference between revealing the latest claim and collecting the entire pile. Wobblox explains the cost of challenging an honest claim. Show the claimed rank and quantity while the cards are face down, and label the declarer, challenger and collector when the claim resolves. Venatus follows its existing same-rank street variant. [SnackTurn guided Bluff game](https://snackturn.com/games/bluff/) · [Wobblox digital Cheat game](https://wobblox.com/games/cheat)

## Chromatic

Mattel163 describes individual action-card effects, stacking and collecting a total penalty. Ubisoft’s console manual distinguishes the available house-rule modes. Show the current colour, direction, amount owed and legal response together. Venatus accepts +2 on +2 and +4 on either +2 or +4. [Mattel163 digital game help](https://prod-letsplayuno.mattel163.com/support/rules.html) · [Ubisoft UNO console manual](https://dlassets-ssl.xboxlive.com/public/content/36bf346f-23ff-4df8-850e-94530fb1b71b/GameManual/4747b7fc-d7c7-4568-bbf9-c16f14e088af/en-AU/index.html)
